DuPont Analysis Explained: 3-Step vs 5-Step ROE, with a Worked Example

ROE went up. Is that good news? DuPont analysis answers the question by splitting return on equity into the pieces that drive it. You can then see whether the company became more profitable, used its assets better, or simply borrowed more.

The 3-step DuPont formula

ROE = Net margin × Asset turnover × Equity multiplier

FactorFormulaQuestion it answers
Net marginNet income ÷ RevenueHow much profit from each unit of sales?
Asset turnoverRevenue ÷ Average total assetsHow hard do the assets work?
Equity multiplierAverage total assets ÷ Average equityHow much of the asset base is funded by debt?

Multiply the three and revenue and assets cancel out, leaving net income ÷ average equity, which is ROE.

Worked example: ROE up from 16% to 20%

Year 1Year 2
Revenue100.0105.0
Net income8.08.4
Average total assets80.084.0
Average equity50.042.0
Net margin8.0%8.0%
Asset turnover1.25×1.25×
Equity multiplier1.60×2.00×
ROE16.0%20.0%

Margin and turnover did not move. The whole rise in ROE came from the equity multiplier going from 1.6× to 2.0×. Equity fell while assets grew, so the extra assets were funded by debt.

Reading: ROE rose because of leverage, not because the business improved. For a lender this is a warning sign, not good news.

The 5-step DuPont formula

The 5-step version breaks net margin into three parts, which separates operating performance from financing and tax effects:

ROE = Tax burden × Interest burden × EBIT margin × Asset turnover × Equity multiplier

FactorFormulaMeaning
Tax burdenNet income ÷ Pre-tax incomeShare of pre-tax profit kept after tax.
Interest burdenPre-tax income ÷ EBITShare of operating profit left after interest. Lower means heavier interest.
EBIT marginEBIT ÷ RevenueOperating profitability.

Year 1 in 5 steps

EBIT 13.0; interest 2.0; pre-tax income 11.0; tax 3.0; net income 8.0.

  • Tax burden = 8.0 ÷ 11.0 = 0.727
  • Interest burden = 11.0 ÷ 13.0 = 0.846
  • EBIT margin = 13.0 ÷ 100.0 = 13.0%
  • Asset turnover = 1.25×; equity multiplier = 1.60×
  • ROE = 0.727 × 0.846 × 0.130 × 1.25 × 1.60 = 16.0%, the same as the 3-step result
Always reconcile. The product of the factors must equal ROE calculated directly. If it doesn't, the inputs are inconsistent: for example, averages were mixed with closing balances, or net income doesn't match the statements. Fix the data before you interpret anything.

How to read DuPont in practice

  • Margin up: better pricing or cost control. Check whether it is sustainable.
  • Turnover up: more sales from the same assets. Check for asset write-downs, which also raise turnover.
  • Equity multiplier up: more leverage. Check interest coverage and net debt / EBITDA.
  • Interest burden down: interest is eating a bigger share of operating profit.
  • Negative equity: ROE and the equity multiplier stop being meaningful. Report them as not meaningful rather than as a number.
